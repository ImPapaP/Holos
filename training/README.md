# Training Gabay's custom models

Open `Gabay_custom_models.ipynb` in Google Colab (File > Upload notebook, or open from GitHub).

- **Custom street detector**: stairs, curbs, holes, open canals, poles, tricycles, jeepneys.
- **Peso bill classifier**: 20, 50, 100, 200, 500, 1000 pesos.

Then place the four output files in `assets/models/` and `assets/labels/` and push. The app loads them automatically.
